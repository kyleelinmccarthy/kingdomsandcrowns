# Blind trail pass — trail-g912 (band g912)

<!-- trail-blind-pass sheet v1 · pool: trail-g912 · clues: 120 -->

**Do not open `src/content/trails/trail-g912.json`** — not before you start, not to
check yourself partway. This sheet is worth exactly as much as that rule is kept.

Each clue below is a note in a village. A hero reads it and walks to the one place it tells of.
Answer each clue ON ITS OWN, as a careful reader at the level of its words would: the clues are
shuffled and unrelated, and a clue never leans on the one above it. After `Answer:` on each line
write one of:

- a place id from the list below, such as `landmark:mill` — the one place the clue tells of.
- `both` and then the ids that also fit, such as `both landmark:well object:water-trough` — more
  than one place fits. This is as much a defect as a wrong key, and it is the one a reader skips
  past when they are only hunting for mistakes, so use it whenever it is true.
- `?` — no place fits, or you cannot tell from the clue alone.

Leave the `#` codes alone. They pin each answer to the clue you actually read, so that if a clue
is edited afterwards the stale answer is reported rather than trusted.

## The places

Every one is in the village, and looks the same to every hero. These facts are always true.

- `landmark:well` — the well. The Village Well gives clean water for every doorstep. Old Bram looks after it. Near: `landmark:mill`.
- `landmark:mill` — the mill. The Grain Mill makes flour for the baker's ovens. Miller Tessa looks after it. Near: `landmark:well`.
- `landmark:bridge` — the bridge. The River Bridge is a crossing that holds in any weather. Carpenter Aldo looks after it. Near: `landmark:market`, `landmark:watchtower`.
- `landmark:chapel` — the chapel. The Chapel is a quiet place with a bell that carries. Sister Wren looks after it. Near: `landmark:library`.
- `landmark:market` — the market. The Market Square has stalls, songs and the smell of bread. Crier Pip looks after it. Near: `landmark:watchtower`, `landmark:bridge`, `landmark:castle-gate`.
- `landmark:library` — the library. The Library keeps every scroll in the realm, shelved and safe. Librarian Hesper looks after it. Near: `landmark:castle-gate`, `landmark:chapel`, `landmark:garden`.
- `landmark:watchtower` — the watchtower. The Watchtower has eyes on the hills and a lantern at night. Mason Gerd looks after it. Near: `landmark:market`, `landmark:bridge`.
- `landmark:garden` — the garden. The Royal Garden grows herbs and keeps bees, with a bench in the sun. Keeper Ivy looks after it. Near: `landmark:library`.
- `landmark:castle-gate` — the castle gate. The way into the castle, at the north end of the road. Near: `landmark:library`, `landmark:market`.
- `landmark:puzzle-tower` — the Puzzle Tower. A tall stone tower of bonus puzzles, at the south-east edge of the village. Near: nothing close.
- `object:notice-board` — the notice board. Villagers pin up news and notes here. It stands by the village's southern entrance. Near: nothing close.
- `object:water-trough` — the water trough. Animals drink here. It is a long wooden trough filled with water, at the south edge of the village. Near: nothing close.
- `object:beehives` — the beehives. Bees live here and make honey. The hives stand at the south-west edge of the village. Near: nothing close.
- `object:hay-cart` — the hay cart. A farm cart piled with hay. It stands at the east edge of the village. Near: nothing close.
